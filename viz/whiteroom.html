CivSYS · experiment setup

Design the life, the chamber and what comes after

Your subject lives a normal life among several villages. One night he is sealed in a white chamber while the world outside falls apart. When the door finally opens he walks into what is left. Every part of that is yours to set. Start from a preset, then change anything.

How it works

Choose a preset or set everything by hand. Then press Begin.

Use the speed buttons and the skip buttons to move through long stretches. In the chamber and afterwards, switch between His mind (what he sees, hallucinations included) and Reality (what a camera would record).
