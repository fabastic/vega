    Does it really hallucinate?

    Yes. Over the 120 blind ticks it reports a person where there is only an object or a wall 0 times, loses sight of a real person 0 times, and swaps walls and objects 0 times (each count is one direction at one tick). Its body drifts too: it comes to feel far thirstier and sleepier than it is, feels pain it does not have, and believes it is lying down while it stands.

    What are the dots in the brain?

    Each dot is one recurrent unit of the model's state. Blue is positive, violet negative, and brightness is how strongly it fires. The ring of larger dots in the core is the 16-number latent it uses to summarise the moment.

    What does cutting the senses do?

    From t=420 the model no longer gets observations. It still gets the actions the agent really took, and it feeds its own decoded prediction back in as if it were what it saw. It is the closest thing this model has to dreaming.

    Is this real data?

    Yes. Every frame is exported by viz/extract_brain.py from the frozen life-trained model on a held-out stream. The sentences above are translated from those numbers by fixed rules; the model itself does not speak.
