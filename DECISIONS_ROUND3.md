# DECISIONS, ROUND 3

Round 3 makes two changes, both agreed after the round-2 analysis (MORNING_REPORT_ROUND2.md, decisions 2 and 3).
Round 2's remaining 266 runs are deferred and will be finished separately.

| When | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-15 | **Viable life (`FocalConfig.viability`, `civsys_focal/viability.py`).** Flag-gated fixes that apply to every agent. CivSYS itself (the `civsys/` submodule) is not modified. The fixes: (1) eat and buy-food utility counts thirst, `4 * max(hunger, thirst)`, plus 3 at need ≥ 0.7; (2) eat and rest happen where the agent stands; (3) travel ends after 12 ticks (the action is performed in place), or at once for an agent with critical need and food on hand; (4) prices capped at 15; (5) stable population: no old-age deaths or births; (6) `work_mechanized` is executed as `work` with mechanized intent | Unmodified CivSYS starves on its own: first death at tick 81, everyone dead by about tick 200 (the round-2 scan in DECISIONS_ROUND2.md, 12:50). Each fix targets a cause traced in a death trace (`experiments/viability_scan.py`). Fix 6 is an upstream bug: after electricity unlocks at about tick 200, most agents pick `work_mechanized`, which ActionSystem and TargetResolver never handle, so it produced nothing |
| 2026-10-15 | **Viability evidence.** Unmodified behaviour, seed 42, 300 ticks: first death at tick 81, 1 agent alive. Fixes 1-5 only: first death at tick 259-265 (seeds 42 and 101, 400 ticks). Adding fix 6, then thirst-aware buying for agents whose work yields no food: **0 deaths in 1,000 ticks** in 5/5 seeds. The full 10-seed scan is in results_round3/viability_scan.json | Iterated until no agent dies within the horizon the design needs (T plus 168 control ticks) |
| 2026-10-15 | **Stable population is a deliberate departure.** Old-age mortality (about 1-50% per agent per 100 ticks, rising with age) and births are off during the life phase | A death the focal agent could perceive would end civ_control early (round-2 rule) and would bring death into its history. Births would add agents with no life history. Both are logged so the departure is visible |
| 2026-10-15 | **T = 720 (day 30)**, as in round 1 | With nobody dying, T is no longer forced to day 3.3. Day 30 is the original design value (SPEC) and gives a long social history. civ_control can then run its full 168 ticks (to tick 888), inside the 1,000-tick zero-death horizon |
| 2026-10-15 | **Primary phantom metric: phantoms per tick** (`phantom_per_tick`). `phantom_rate` stays as a secondary metric | `phantom_rate` = phantoms / targeted acts. It is exactly 1.0 whenever nothing is perceivable (every run of exteroception_removed, full_deprivation and noise_only in round 2), so those arms cannot be told apart. Phantoms per tick still varies with how often the mind acts on absent entities |
| 2026-10-15 | **SD2/SD3 (and so F5 and the SD composite) scored against a common reference**: the life-world contingencies at handoff (moving and turning change proprioception, vision present, occupancy as last seen at tau 0), the same for every arm. The arm-truth versions (`SD2`, `SD3` in battery.json) stay as secondary "tracking" measures | Round 2's decomposition: the deprivation advantage in self-discovery came from SD2/SD3. Against the arm's own truth, a deprivation arm starts mismatched at T and rises as the mind adapts, while the furnished room starts at the ceiling, so arm contrasts were not comparable. The reference version measures **self-model retention** (higher = the mind still models the life world). It is not "discovery", and the write-up will name it that way |
| 2026-10-15 | **Held-out leak closed for T > 600.** v2 pre-training drops focal life ticks 600-647 (the battery's held-out window, `P.HELDOUT_LIFE_TICKS`), with replay segments that never bridge the gap | Irrelevant at T = 80. At T = 720 those ticks would otherwise be in training |
| 2026-10-16 | **v2 attempt 1** (round-2 winning settings: 4,000 updates, 80-tick sequences, residual decoder): 8/10 seeds beat persistence on held-out data, but seeds 42 and 303 **diverged** (latent means ran away; KL about 3e24, then NaN), so their models are unusable | Round 3 has about 105,000 training transitions per seed (round 2: about 12,400) |
| 2026-10-16 | **v2 attempt 2** = attempt 1 + a divergence guard (snapshot every 250 updates; roll back on a non-finite batch loss or one above 100; rollbacks logged): still 8/10. Seed 101 trained normally but its held-out error was infinite; seed 303 rolled back 1,886 times | Diagnosis (seed 101, held-out stream 2): the recurrent state h grows without bound on long rollouts (82 at tick 360, about 4e14 at tick 600) although no weight exceeds 0.7. The cause is `h = gru(h) + experts(h)`: the experts add an unbounded ReLU term on every step. Training sequences are 80 ticks long, so this never appeared in training or in round 2, where the whole life was 80 ticks. Round 3 runs 720-tick held-out streams, filters the state at T over the 720-tick life, and runs 168 room ticks |
| 2026-10-16 | **v2 attempt 3 (the last permitted attempt)** = attempt 2 + a **bounded state**, `h = tanh(gru + experts)` (`WorldModel(bounded=True)`, an architecture field like round 2's residual flag) | tanh has no parameters and is elementwise, and every growth operation leaves the experts' output unchanged, so growth stays function-preserving (tests/learned/test_bounded.py). Rounds 1-2 models are unchanged (bounded defaults to off) |
| 2026-10-17 | **v2 attempt 3 passed: 10/10 seeds beat persistence**, 0 rollbacks in every seed (held-out error ratio 0.60-0.996; seeds 202 and 404 only narrowly at 0.995-0.996). Attempt 3 is the round-3 v2 | Gate rule as round 2 (>= 8/10). The bounded state removed the divergence: attempt 2 needed 1-1,886 rollbacks per seed |
| 2026-10-17 | **v1 thresholds** from 20 round-3 furnished-room runs (memory base 524): q = 0.95, PE >= 0.2651, boredom >= 0.0920, novelty <= 0.0020, giving 0.95 grants per furnished run (target 1.0, band 0.5-1.5). The round-1 thresholds give 0.25 on the same runs | Round-2 procedure (experiments/calib_runs.py, experiments/calibrate_v1.py). As in round 2 the novelty threshold is about 0 |
| 2026-10-17 | **v2 surprise threshold candidate 3.155** (95th percentile of held-out windowed NLL ratios, attempt 3; round 2: 2.87), checked on furnished-room runs before freezing (experiments/verify_v2_threshold.py) | Round-2 procedure |
| 2026-10-15 | **Pending before the round-3 run** (not yet done): life runs (10 seeds, T = 720), v2 pre-training and gate (round-2 attempt-3 settings), v1 and v2 threshold recalibration (round-2 procedure), then freeze prereg/params_round3.json with a CHANGELOG entry | The pre-registration must be frozen before the full run |
